We invite you to the ninth scientific and technical conference of the radio‑electronic industry,
which will be held in the city of Sochi!
The business agenda will cover key factors and strategic plans for the development of the Russian
electronic industry, product segments and technological directions, prospects for creating new industrial
enterprises and reconstructing existing ones.
From the reports by the key speakers at this event, you will learn about measures of state support for manufacturers,
about investment and financial resources for the development of the electronic industry,
and about the opportunities for public‑private partnership.
We look forward to seeing you at this major Russian forum!
